      Surface cover
      How to judge surface cover
      Vegetation
      Live vegetation projected over the monitored surface.
      Bare ground
      Exposed mineral soil without vegetation, litter, or coarse rock covering it.
      Gravel / rock
      Visible gravel, rubble, or rock covering the surface.
      Litter / mulch
      Detached leaves, stems, mulch, or wood chips on the ground.
      Standing dead
      Upright attached dead vegetation. It may overlap the ground-surface categories.

      Estimate the first four categories to about 100% only when the framing shows the full monitored surface. Leave unsupported values blank.

      Taxa
      How to label taxa and reproductive state
      Dominant / secondary / tertiary
      Order taxa by visible live cover, not by photo order or visual interest.
      Rank
      Use species only with diagnostic evidence; otherwise use genus, family, unknown, or a stable morphotype such as unknown_grass_01.
      Confidence
      Confident: diagnostic evidence is clear. Likely: good fit with some uncertainty. Uncertain: tentative working label.
      Flowering
      Yes only when open flowers are visible. Use Unknown when the relevant structures are not visible.
      Seed set
      Yes only when fruit or seed structures are visible. Use Unknown when the photos cannot resolve it.

      Interpretation and completeness
      How to judge interpretation and review status
      New taxon detected
      Yes only when comparison with earlier records supports that the taxon was not previously recorded at this site.
      Early colonization
      Mostly open substrate with scattered pioneer plants.
      Developing herb layer
      Herbaceous cover is expanding but remains discontinuous.
      Dense herb layer
      Herbaceous vegetation is mostly continuous.
      Shrub establishment
      Persistent woody shrubs are becoming structurally important.
      Young woodland
      Young trees form the developing vegetation structure.
      Reviewed
      All evidence available in this batch was assessed; fields may still be blank when unknowable.
      Partial
      A relevant source or part of the batch could not be assessed. State what is missing in the completeness note.
      Observation note
      Visible conditions, uncertainty, or context. Do not infer an unrecorded disturbance.
      Completeness note
      Why the review is partial, such as missing framing or an unreadable close-up.
